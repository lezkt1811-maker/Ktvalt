🛡️ What “no quality loss” means here

This app uploads each selected photo as a raw binary file. It never decodes and re-encodes the image. A SHA-256 fingerprint is computed locally and becomes the archive ID, so uploading the same exact photo again does not create another full copy. The original filename, MIME type, modification date and byte size are saved as metadata.
